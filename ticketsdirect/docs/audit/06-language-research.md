# Language research: German, Italian, French

Written 7 November 2026, before building the DE/IT/FR versions, as the owner asked: localise to what each market actually searches, not a literal translation. Sources: the German pages of OK Lanzarote (which rank and carry FAQ markup), the Italian and French Civitatis Lanzarote pages, and Google searches in each language. Terms below are how these ranking pages phrase things — use them in titles, headings, category names and meta.

## Title / meta patterns that rank

- German: "Die besten [Thema]-Touren Lanzarotes 2026" and "[Thema] Lanzarote: Die besten Touren & Ausflüge".
- French: "Les meilleures excursions … à Lanzarote" / "Excursion à [lieu] : …".
- Italian: "Le migliori escursioni … a Lanzarote" / "Escursione a [luogo] : …".

All three markets respond to **the year (2026)**, **"die besten / les meilleures / le migliori"**, and **free cancellation** in the snippet.

## Top customer questions per language (for the FAQ blocks)

These mirror the English set but in each market's own wording; the pickup/price/duration questions dominate everywhere.

**German** (from OK Lanzarote's FAQ):
- Ist der Transfer / die Abholung inbegriffen, und wo?
- Wie lange dauert der Ausflug?
- Beinhaltet die Timanfaya-Tour den Kamelritt?
- Was sollte ich anziehen / mitbringen?
- Organisierter Ausflug oder auf eigene Faust?
- Ist die Tour für Kinder geeignet? Ab welchem Alter?

**French** (from Civitatis + search):
- Le transfert / la prise en charge est-il inclus, et où ?
- Combien de temps dure l'excursion ?
- D'où part le ferry pour La Graciosa ? (Órzola)
- L'annulation est-elle gratuite ?
- L'excursion est-elle adaptée aux enfants ?
- Quelle est la meilleure période ? (vent plus faible d'avril à octobre)

**Italian** (from Civitatis + search):
- Il trasferimento / transfer è incluso, e da dove?
- Quanto dura l'escursione?
- Da dove parte il traghetto per La Graciosa? (Órzola)
- La cancellazione è gratuita?
- Il voucher va stampato o va bene sul telefono?
- È adatta ai bambini? Da che età?

## How this feeds the build

1. **Category names, resort labels and meta** use the glossary terms above (not a word-for-word translation of the English).
2. **Titles** follow each market's ranking pattern, with the year and "best".
3. **The common FAQ block** (group discounts, offers, cancellation, pickup, duration) is written in each language using the questions above, so the pages can be quoted by Google/AI in that language.
4. **Tour descriptions** (the long tail, ~107k words) fall back to **English** at first — international visitors read English, and this avoids shipping rough machine translation. Staff translate/approve the top tours per language before launch; the rest are translated in batches after.

This keeps the first preview honest: fully localised structure, navigation, titles, meta and FAQs, with tour bodies in English until a native speaker has approved them.
