# Google Business Profile kit: Tickets Direct Excursions Lanzarote

Generated 2026-10-24 from the live catalogue (83 excursions). Re-run `npm run gbp` after prices change.

Your 10 profiles are the fastest way to beat the small agencies: Google shows the "map pack" above every organic result for searches like *excursions Costa Teguise* or *boat trips Playa Blanca*, and the big platforms (GetYourGuide, Civitatis) have no offices there, so they can't appear in it. Ranking in the map pack depends on **relevance** (categories, services, description), **distance** (you have offices in all three resorts) and **prominence** (reviews, photos, activity). This kit covers all three.

## 1. Do this on every profile

| Field | What to set |
|---|---|
| Business name | Your real trading name, the same everywhere: **Tickets Direct Excursions** + the location you use on the shopfront (e.g. "Tickets Direct Excursions Playa Bastián"). Don't add keywords like "Best Boat Trips": Google suspends profiles for it. |
| Primary category | **Tour agency** |
| Secondary categories | Travel agency · Boat tour agency · Sightseeing tour agency · Tourist information center · Ticket office (only those that are true for that office) |
| Phone | The office's own number if it has one, otherwise +34 633 229 244. Add WhatsApp under *Chat*. |
| Website | The resort page with tracking (links below), not the homepage, so each office ranks for its own area. |
| Booking link | https://excursionsticketsdirect.com/trip/ (Google shows a "Book" button) |
| Hours | Real hours, plus holiday hours for Christmas, New Year and Easter. Profiles with missing or wrong hours lose ranking. |
| Opening date | 2000 (or the year that office opened) |
| Attributes | Languages: English, Spanish (+ German/French if staff speak them) · Accepts credit cards · Online appointments/bookings · Wheelchair accessible entrance (if true) · LGBTQ+ friendly (if you want it shown) |
| Service area | Lanzarote, plus La Graciosa and Fuerteventura |
| Description | Copy the 750-character description for the resort below (EN as main; Google auto-translates, but add the ES text as an *Update* too) |

**NAP consistency:** name, address and phone must match exactly across the profile, the website's offices page (`src/data/offices.json`), Tripadvisor, Facebook and directories. Mismatches are one of the most common local-ranking problems.
